Keeping things secure is super important to us at our organization. To make sure we're staying on top of security best practices, we're rolling out an automatic 90-day warning and rotation system for AWS IAM User Access Keys.

Why does this matter to you?
Rotating IAM credentials regularly helps prevent unauthorized access if a key ever gets compromised. This simple step can make a huge difference in securing your AWS resources.

What’s changing?
The system will automatically rotate non-compliant keys. This means you’ll need to update your applications with new Access Key pairs every 90 days. These keys will be stored in AWS Secrets Manager, with access restricted to your application’s IAM User. After rotation, you’ll have a 10-day grace period before old Access Keys are disabled, followed by another 10 days before deletion. During this time, you can still use old keys to retrieve new ones from Secrets Manager.

By enabling this, you align with compliance standards such as SOC2, the Center of Internet Security AWS Foundations Benchmark, APRA, MAS, NIST, and the AWS Well-Architected Framework.
